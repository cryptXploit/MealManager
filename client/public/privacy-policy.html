Privacy Policy for Meal Manager

Last Updated: September 21, 2026

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at Meal Manager. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy notice, or our practices with regards to your personal information, please contact us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the App, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products and Services, when you participate in activities on the App, or otherwise when you contact us.

  • Personal Information Provided by You: We collect names; email addresses; passwords; and other similar information.
  • App Data: We collect data related to your mess/group management, including daily meal counts, bazaar (grocery) expenses, deposit amounts, and member activity logs to provide the core functionality of the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information collected via our App for a variety of business purposes described below:

  • To facilitate account creation and logon process.
  • To provide and manage the core features: Calculating meal rates, managing expenses, and showing group statistics.
  • To save and download reports: Generating and downloading PDF reports of your meal charts.

3. Will Your Information Be Shared With Anyone?

We only share information with the following third parties:

  • Supabase: We use Supabase as our backend database and authentication provider. Your data is securely stored on their servers.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with any third parties for promotional purposes.

4. Is Your Information Transferred Internationally?

Our servers are hosted by Supabase. If you are accessing our App from outside the region where our servers are located, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed by us in our facilities and by those third parties with whom we may share your personal information.

5. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

We will only keep your personal information for as long as it is necessary for the purposes set out in this privacy notice, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law (such as tax, accounting or other legal requirements). You may request deletion of your account and data at any time.

6. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

We have implemented appropriate technical and organizational security measures (such as Row Level Security in our database) designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. However, despite our safeguards and efforts to secure your information, no electronic transmission over the Internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this notice, you may email us at the developer contact email provided on our Google Play Store listing.
